Privacy

Last updated 10 October 2026

Playing as a guest

Your room, Hearts, character and clothes are saved only in your browser on this device. Nothing is sent to us.

Signing in with Google

If you sign in, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with PocketRoom. We use them only to show who is signed in and to keep your save on your account, so you can play on any device. Your game save is stored with our database provider, Supabase, and only your account can read or change it.

Weather

If you choose to see your real weather, your location or the city you pick stays in your browser. It is sent only to the free Open-Meteo weather service to get the forecast.

What we don't do

  • No ads, and no selling or sharing your information.
  • No real-money purchases.
  • PocketRoom is for ages 13 and up.

Deleting your data

Clear your browser's site data to remove a guest save. To delete your account and its save, email roghpeduca@gmail.com.
